# DICOM course for personnel working with nordicMEDiVA

This DICOM course is useful for any employee or personnel working with nordicMEDiVA, such as
* Onboarding team
* Support personnel
* (New) software engineers
* Software Quality assurance engineers

Or just anyone who's interested in learning some details about the DICOM format.

## How to do the course
### In a group, led by an expert (recommended)
The recommended way to do this course is through workshops together with co-workers. This can either be led by a DICOM expert in the organization, or you can go through it together as a group. The exercises encourage discussion, and, as of now, these discussions should be had with a DICOM expert to understand if you are on the right track or not.

### Alone
If you want, however, you can do this course on your own by going through the modules in order. If you have prior knowledge, you may also skip some modules at your own pace, but keep in mind that you may have missed some important lectures from the previous modules.

Also, since the exercises encourage discussions, there is sometimes not a right or wrong answer, so you will miss out on some important concepts if you don't have an expert to guide you.
